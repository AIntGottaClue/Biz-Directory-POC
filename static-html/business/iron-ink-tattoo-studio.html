About Iron Ink Tattoo Studio

Iron Ink Tattoo Studio is Round Rock's premier custom tattoo and piercing studio, home to a collective of award-winning artists specializing in black-and-grey realism, traditional American, fine-line, and Japanese-style tattooing. Our private, hospital-grade sterilized studio features artist suites designed for comfort and creativity. We offer free consultations where you'll collaborate directly with your artist to design a one-of-a-kind piece, and we use only premium vegan inks and top-tier aftercare products. Beyond tattoos, we provide safe, professional body piercing with implant-grade titanium jewelry. Whether it's your first tattoo or a full sleeve, our artists deliver clean, lasting artwork in a welcoming, inclusive space.

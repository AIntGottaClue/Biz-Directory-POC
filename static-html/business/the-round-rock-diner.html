About The Round Rock Diner

The Round Rock Diner is a beloved local institution serving classic American comfort food since 1998. Famous for our hand-pressed burgers, chicken-fried steak, all-day breakfast, and the legendary homemade pies baked fresh each morning. Our retro counter and booth seating, friendly servers, and generous portions make every meal feel like home. We source produce from nearby Texas farms whenever possible and brew locally roasted coffee all day long. Stop by for lunch, bring the family for dinner, or grab a slice of buttermilk pie to go — we've been the neighborhood gathering spot for over 25 years.
